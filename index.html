Rules

  • Week 7 decision: all 3×2000 m at 4:45–4:50 with HR ≤ 178 → go for 47:30. Slower than 4:52 or HR above 180 → race for 48:00.
  • Heavy legs: drop the Wednesday run first, then the Thursday reps, then make Tuesday easy.
  • Failed workout: finish 5 s/km slower, don't repeat it, move on.
  • Sharp or one-sided pain: stop, skip the next run, see a physio if it lasts 3 days.
  • Missed week: repeat the previous week. Two missed weeks → aim for 48:00.
  • Half marathon (1 Nov): 5:30/km, HR around 165, slow down above 168. With pain or heavy legs that week, skip it or stop at 15 km.
  • Shoes: Endorphin Speed 5 for the week 7 session and the 10K; run the half in your usual long-run shoes. Check wear on the two ~540 km easy pairs.
